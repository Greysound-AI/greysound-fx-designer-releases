## Greysound FX Designer 0.2.0

- **Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand.** On macOS the installer now adds an Audio Unit next to the VST3, so FX Designer works in Logic Pro and GarageBand as well as your other DAWs.
- **Bypass buttons.** Every effect and every chain has a power button. A bypassed effect passes its input through unchanged; a bypassed chain drops out of the mix. Switches fade in and out without clicks, can be undone, are saved with your project, and stay as you set them when you ask the chat for changes.
- **Light and dark mode.** Switch between them with the sun and moon button in the header. FX Designer remembers your choice and opens in it.
- **A clearer start.** Before you sign in, FX Designer shows your options side by side, with the real prices: subscribe to FX Designer on its own, or use any Greysound plan, which includes this plugin at no extra cost. If you already have a Greysound plan, sign in with that account.
- **Manage billing** now opens a page with your current plan and the Greysound plans first, with Stripe one click away.
- Bypassed effects no longer lengthen your bounces with tails nobody hears.
- The chat box has tidier spacing, and the account menu matches the rest of Greysound.
