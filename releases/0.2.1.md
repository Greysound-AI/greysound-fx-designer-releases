## Greysound FX Designer 0.2.1

- **Windows: nothing to install by hand.** FX Designer no longer depends on the Visual C++ runtime installed on your PC, which could crash the plug-in or your DAW on PCs with an older copy. The installer also sets up Microsoft's WebView2 Runtime when your PC does not have it.
- **Manage billing names your plan.** With a Greysound plan, the page now shows which one is active, for example "Studio", with its billing period and weekly credits.
